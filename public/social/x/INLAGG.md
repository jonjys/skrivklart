### 6 – `en1-sambo.jpg`
Moving in with your partner in Sweden? Under Swedish cohabitation law, a home bought to live in together is split 50/50 if you break up – no matter who paid.

### 7 – `en2-inherit.jpg`
Unmarried couples in Sweden don't inherit from each other. Not after 5 years, not after 25.

A cohabitation agreement covers a breakup. A will covers death. Most couples need both.
